How It Works

Simple for sellers.
Powerful for buyers.

Whether you're clearing out a renovation site or running a professional scrap yard, ScrapX streamlines every step.

👤 For Sellers
1

Take 3 photos

Snap a front, side, and close-up of your scrap. The app guides you through each shot.

2

AI identifies your metal

Gemini-powered analysis detects metal type, grade, condition, and estimated weight in seconds.

3

Compare nearby yards

See a live map of verified scrap yards with today's condition-adjusted price per kg.

4

Deliver & get paid

Drop off or request pickup. Weight confirmed on-site — payout hits your wallet instantly.

🏭 For Scrap Yards
1

Register your yard

Complete business verification (KYB), add your facilities, and go live on the marketplace.

2

Set your daily prices

Use auto market pricing tied to live LME rates, or manually set buy prices per metal and grade each morning.

3

Accept incoming requests

Review seller submissions with photos and AI assessments. Auto-accept or manually approve.

4

Confirm & build inventory

Weigh metal on arrival, confirm condition, release payment. Stockpile updates automatically.

Features

Everything in one app

Built from the ground up for the scrap metal industry — not a generic marketplace.

AI Metal Identification

Gemini-powered image analysis identifies metal type, grade, surface condition, and estimated weight — in under 10 seconds.

KYC & KYB Verified

Every seller is identity-verified (KYC) and every buyer undergoes business verification (KYB) before their first transaction.

Live Yard Map

Real-time map showing verified scrap yards, their today's price per metal type and grade, distance, and pickup availability.

Instant Wallet Payout

Funds released to your ScrapX wallet the moment the yard confirms the weigh-in. Withdraw to your registered bank account anytime.

Business Dashboard

Yard operators get a full pricing desk, auto/manual acceptance modes, live inventory tracking, and multi-yard management.

Multilingual Support

Full support for Latvian, Lithuanian, Estonian, and English — built for the Baltic market from day one.

About ScrapX

Who we are

Company

ScrapX SIA — registered in Latvia, operating across the Baltic states.

Platform

iOS and Android mobile application with seller, buyer, and business portals.

Market

Latvia, Lithuania, and Estonia — with planned expansion across the EU.

Compliance

GDPR compliant. KYC/KYB via licensed identity verification partners. Payment processing through EU-regulated PSPs.

ScrapX was founded to modernise an industry that still runs on phone calls, cash, and guesswork. The scrap metal market in the Baltics is worth hundreds of millions of euros annually — yet most transactions between private sellers and recycling facilities happen with no price transparency, no verification, and no digital record.

We built ScrapX to change that. By combining AI-powered metal identification, a verified two-sided marketplace, and integrated payment infrastructure, we make it possible for anyone to sell scrap metal safely, transparently, and at a fair price.

Our platform is designed with compliance at its core. Every user is verified, every transaction is recorded, and every payment is processed through regulated channels — giving payment partners, regulators, and compliance teams the transparency they need.
